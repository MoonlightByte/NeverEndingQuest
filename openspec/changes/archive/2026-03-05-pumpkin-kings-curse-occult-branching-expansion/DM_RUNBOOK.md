## Quick Reference: Five Valid Endings

| Ending | Difficulty | Preparation Required | Combat Challenge | Moral Weight |
|--------|------------|---------------------|------------------|--------------|
| **END001: Bramble Sacrifice** | Medium-High | Investigation + NPC Ally | Reduced (King bound) | Heavy (life lost) |
| **END002: Contract Void** | Medium | Investigation + Items | Reduced (King weakened) | Light (contract destroyed) |
| **END003: Kingslayer** | Low | None | Extreme (King full power) | Neutral (combat only) |
| **END004: Dark Bargain** | N/A | Automatic offer | Avoided (evil choice) | Damning (party corrupted) |
| **END005: Collective Refusal** | High | Social + Investigation | Minimal (King powerless) | Communal (village united) |

**Parity Principle:** Investigation/social investment reduces combat difficulty. Combat-only path is hardest. No "correct" ending—each represents different philosophy on cost vs. redemption.

## Ending 1: The Bramble Sacrifice (END001)

### Unlock Requirements
- **Executioner's Sickle** from GRV001 (Gallows Oak hollow)
- **Bramble descendant willing to sacrifice** (Mayor Tilda or revealed kin)
- **Knowledge of contract clause** (BOO001 pact fragment OR GRV001 Judge dialogue)

### Trigger Condition
Present sickle + willing Bramble + speak chant: "By the gardener's hand, the tree is cut."

### Combat Effects
- Pumpkin King is **bound and restrained** for 2 rounds
- King loses **regeneration** for remainder of combat
- King's **HP reduced** to 250 (from 350)
- **No lair actions** while bound

### Narrative Consequences
- Sacrificing Bramble dies irrevocably
- Curse broken permanently
- Village honors the fallen as hero
- Bramble line extinguished (if Mayor dies)

### DM Guidance
- Foreshadow through GRV001 Judge: "The gardener's axe must be willingly wielded"
- Let players discover Tilda's lineage through CMS001/VO001 clues
- Emotional weight: sacrifice must be willing (coercion fails)
- Alternative: If Tilda has revealed kin, they may volunteer

## Ending 2: Contract Void (END002)

### Unlock Requirements
- **Discovery of crown secret** (HLF001 Arcana DC 16 OR BOO001 clues from Altar Keeper)
- **Seven toys** from CMS001 (one from each lost decade)
- **Knowledge that contract is stored in crown** (CMS001 X01 Elric journal)

### Trigger Condition
Target crown specifically during combat + destroy it (AC 12, HP 20)

### Combat Effects
- King's **contract voided**—he loses claim to harvested souls
- King's **HP reduced** to 280
- **Regeneration halved** (5 HP/round)
- **Fear aura suppressed** (DC reduced to 10)

### Narrative Consequences
- Seven children's souls freed immediately
- Harvest Witnesses vanish (grateful, not angry)
- Curse broken but King remains dangerous
- King becomes desperate, fights recklessly

### DM Guidance
- Crown is obvious—flaming jack-o'-lanterns arranged as crown
- Arcana DC 16 reveals names carved inside each pumpkin
- Players may use called shots to target crown (-2 attack penalty)
- Once destroyed, describe souls rising and King's rage

## Ending 3: The Kingslayer (END003)

### Unlock Requirements
- **None**—always available

### Trigger Condition
Defeat Pumpkin King through combat alone without ritual preparation

### Combat Effects
- King fights at **full strength** (350 HP, regeneration 10/round)
- **All lair actions active** (Initiative 20)
- **Wave spawns** at 70% and 30% HP
- **Fear aura** at full DC 13
- **Throne regeneration** active unless destroyed

### Narrative Consequences
- Curse broken through destruction of source
- Contract technically remains—a future threat looms
- Party proven as warriors of legend
- No special after-effects

### DM Guidance
- This is the **baseline fallback** for parties who miss clues
- Emphasize difficulty—King is terrifying at full power
- Allow tactical use of terrain and lair features
- Note in epilogue: "The contract was not destroyed, only its keeper..."

## Ending 4: Dark Bargain (END004)

### Unlock Requirements
- **King makes offer** automatically at confrontation start

### Trigger Condition
Accept the King's offer (requires unanimous party consent or majority vote)

### Combat Effects
- **Combat avoided entirely**
- No XP from boss fight
- Instead: Party gains **Harvester Template** (cosmetic + mechanics)

### Narrative Consequences
- King spares Greenfields Vale
- Party becomes **instruments of the curse**
- Must harvest seven souls from elsewhere within one year
- Campaign continues as "villains" serving the King
- Potential redemption arc in future sessions

### DM Guidance
- Offer presented as genuine mercy: "Seven for seven. Your village lives."
- Emphasize damning nature—players should feel the weight
- If accepted, describe transformation and new purpose
- Sets up future campaign where party hunts targets
- **Not recommended for heroic campaigns**

## Ending 5: Collective Refusal (END005)

### Unlock Requirements
- **Knowledge of collective refusal clause** (BOO001 V14 stone tablet)
- **Seven toys from CMS001** (to awaken all Witnesses)
- **Persuasion DC 17** to unify Witnesses' voices
- **At least 3 villagers rescued** and present

### Trigger Condition
Party leads unified front + speaks refusal in unison: "We do not give. You cannot take."

### Combat Effects
- King's **power drains** over 3 rounds
- **Regeneration stops immediately**
- **Fear aura nullified**
- **Lair actions cease**
- By round 3: King is **helpless** (can be destroyed at advantage)

### Narrative Consequences
- Contract voided through collective will
- Harvest Witnesses freed and grateful
- Villagers become heroes alongside party
- Demonstrates power of community over tyranny
- Lowest combat difficulty, highest social investment

### DM Guidance
- Requires most setup but trivializes combat
- Witnesses must be awakened (seven toys from CMS001)
- Persuasion DC 17 represents unifying traumatized spirits
- Describe growing chorus of refusal weakening the King
- Emphasize empowerment of ordinary people

## Ending Parity Analysis (Task 2.3)

### Viability Balance

| Factor | Sacrifice | Contract Void | Kingslayer | Dark Bargain | Collective Refusal |
|--------|-----------|---------------|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Preparation Effort** | Medium | Medium | None | None | High |
| **Combat Difficulty** | Medium | Medium | Extreme | N/A | Trivial |
| **Social/Investigation** | Low | High | None | None | Extreme |
| **Moral Cost** | Heavy (life) | Light | Neutral | Damning | Communal |
| **Narrative Reward** | Heroic sacrifice | Investigator | Warrior | Corrupted | Community |

### Parity Rationale

**Investigation ↔ Combat Trade-off:**
- Parties investing in investigation find Contract Void or Collective Refusal → easier combat
- Parties skipping investigation face Kingslayer → harder combat
- Balanced: equal party capability required, different distributions

**Sacrifice as Middle Path:**
- Requires moderate investigation (GRV001)
- Requires NPC relationship (Bramble kin)
- Reduces combat but costs life
- Balanced: preparation reduces difficulty but adds moral weight

**Dark Bargain as False Mercy:**
- Easiest path (no combat)
- Highest moral cost
- Story consequence over gameplay consequence
- Balanced: mechanical ease vs. narrative damnation

**No Dominant Strategy:**
- Speedrunners: Kingslayer (hard combat, fast)
- Investigators: Contract Void or Collective Refusal (prep-heavy, easy combat)
- Roleplayers: Sacrifice or Dark Bargain (narrative focused)
- All paths viable, none strictly superior

## Driving Each Ending: DM Playbook

### For Bramble Sacrifice
1. **Foreshadow in HFG001/VO001:** Mayor Tilda's family history
2. **Reveal in GRV001:** Judge's words about "gardener's axe"
3. **Discover weapon:** Executioner's Sickle in Gallows Oak
4. **Build relationship:** Tilda must trust party enough to sacrifice
5. **Trigger moment:** Sickle + willing Bramble + chant

### For Contract Void
1. **Plant clue in CMS001:** Elric's journal mentions crown
2. **Confirm in BOO001:** Altar Keeper Shade confirms contract location
3. **Collect toys in CMS001:** Seven items from cornfields
4. **Investigate in HLF001:** Arcana DC 16 reveals names
5. **Trigger moment:** Called shots to destroy crown

### For Kingslayer
1. **Let players skip investigation:** Always valid
2. **Emphasize difficulty:** "The King regards you with burning contempt"
3. **Support tactical play:** Lair features, throne destruction
4. **Reward victory:** Standard XP, standard loot
5. **Note consequence:** Contract remains for future threats

### For Dark Bargain
1. **Present offer dramatically:** Before combat begins
2. **Make terms clear:** Seven souls for seven
3. **Allow debate:** Party discussion, potential split
4. **Execute transformation:** Describe harvester creation
5. **Continue campaign:** New villainous objectives

### For Collective Refusal
1. **Scatter toys in CMS001:** Seven locations, seven decades
2. **Reveal clause in BOO001:** Stone tablet with counter-ritual
3. **Gather villagers:** Rescue at least 3 during module
4. **Unify Witnesses:** Persuasion DC 17 in HLF001
5. **Trigger moment:** Chorus of refusal, King weakens
