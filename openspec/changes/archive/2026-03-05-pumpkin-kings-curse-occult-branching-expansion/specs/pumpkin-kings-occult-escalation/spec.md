## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The module SHALL escalate restrained occult horror across the existing area progression
The narrative SHALL increase occult dread from HFG001 through HLF001 using implication-first cues, ritual unease, and moral pressure while avoiding explicit gore-forward dependency.

#### Scenario: Early-stage dread in village spaces
- **WHEN** players investigate HFG001 and VO001
- **THEN** they encounter subtle but credible omen and pact-origin signals that suggest hidden corruption beneath festival normalcy

#### Scenario: Mid-stage ritual and memory distortion pressure
- **WHEN** players progress through CMS001 and BOO001
- **THEN** clue content reinforces occult logic, failed prior interventions, and rising consequence pressure

#### Scenario: End-stage confrontation tone consistency
- **WHEN** players reach GRV001 and HLF001
- **THEN** endgame content presents severe moral dread and ritual stakes in restrained language without explicit splatter framing

### Requirement: Origin evidence MUST be discoverable through in-world artifacts
The module MUST provide at least two independent artifact-based pathways to discover pact origin and corruption mechanics.

#### Scenario: Primary origin evidence found
- **WHEN** players complete one artifact trail
- **THEN** they can identify who initiated the pact and why it became predatory

#### Scenario: Redundant origin path available
- **WHEN** players miss one artifact trail
- **THEN** a second trail still allows progression toward ending decisions
