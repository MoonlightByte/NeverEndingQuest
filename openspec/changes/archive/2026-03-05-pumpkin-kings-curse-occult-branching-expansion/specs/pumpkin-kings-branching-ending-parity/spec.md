## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The module SHALL provide multiple equal-viability endings
The finale SHALL support at least four distinct outcomes (Sacrifice, Contract Void, Kingslayer, Dark Bargain) with comparable viability through different play styles.

#### Scenario: Investigation-forward group
- **WHEN** players prioritize clue discovery and ritual reasoning
- **THEN** Contract Void ending is reachable without requiring combat-only optimization

#### Scenario: Combat-forward group
- **WHEN** players prioritize direct confrontation and skip optional clues
- **THEN** Kingslayer ending remains reachable as a valid but high-pressure path

#### Scenario: Moral-cost group
- **WHEN** players accept high moral cost choices
- **THEN** Sacrifice and Dark Bargain endings are reachable with distinct consequences

### Requirement: Ending unlocks MUST be reason-first and in-world legible
Ending paths MUST be unlocked through diegetic clues and explicit player decisions rather than hidden meta requirements.

#### Scenario: Clue-complete branch unlock
- **WHEN** players gather and interpret branch-critical clues
- **THEN** they can intentionally choose among available ending routes

#### Scenario: Partial-clue fallback
- **WHEN** players miss some optional clues
- **THEN** at least one ending remains clearly attainable and the module does not soft-lock progression
