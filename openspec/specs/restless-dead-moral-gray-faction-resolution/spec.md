## Requirements
### Requirement: Three Valid Faction Outcomes
The module SHALL define at least three valid climax outcomes: aid the cult, oppose the cult, or negotiate a third path.

#### Scenario: Outcome set exists and is distinguishable
- **WHEN** the party reaches climax content (NIG06)
- **THEN** narrative metadata includes outcome choices for aid/oppose/negotiate
- **AND** each outcome includes a distinct consequence summary

### Requirement: Moral Grayness Preservation
The module SHALL portray cult leadership as both harmful and motivated by curse-pressure, avoiding one-dimensional framing.

#### Scenario: Faction framing remains mixed
- **WHEN** NPC and context updates are applied
- **THEN** at least one clue shows cult harm to innocents
- **AND** at least one clue shows cult motive rooted in shared curse pressure
- **AND** both signals coexist in module context
