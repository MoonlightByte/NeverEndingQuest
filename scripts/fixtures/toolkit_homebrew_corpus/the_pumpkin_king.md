# The Pumpkin King

The fields around Wick Hollow whisper with old bargains. Lanterns burn all night, but no one leaves the harvest road after dusk.

## Premise

A string of disappearances has rattled the vale. The villagers blame a masked spirit called the Pumpkin King.

## Key Beats

- Follow witness testimony from farmstead to chapel crypt.
- Recover an ember relic needed to break the curse.
- Confront the Pumpkin King in a ritual circle of black corn.
