# The Garden of Demons

The old estate at Blackglass Hill is sealed by thorned iron and prayer-stones, but crimson vines still creep down the road.

## Premise

The party is hired to retrieve a family covenant before cultists can burn it in the garden shrine.

## Key Beats

- Enter the hedge maze and map unstable paths.
- Negotiate with trapped spirits guarding the greenhouse vault.
- Break the infernal root-heart before dawn.
