# A Pottsfield Burial

Pottsfield keeps no bells and no welcome fire. Fresh graves sit open by morning, and no one speaks the names of the dead.

## Premise

A caravan master asks the party to escort a burial train and uncover who is disturbing the cemetery.

## Key Beats

- Track grave-robbers through marsh paths and ossuary tunnels.
- Recover an ancestral register from a flooded crypt archive.
- Resolve the final rite before the moonless vigil ends.
