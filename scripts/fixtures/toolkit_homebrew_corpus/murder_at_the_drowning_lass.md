# Murder at the Drowning Lass

Rain hammers the harbor roof as the watch bars every exit. One patron is dead, another missing, and the tide keeps rising.

## Premise

The party must solve a locked-room tavern murder before a smuggling ring escapes through storm drains.

## Key Beats

- Interview patrons while the scene remains sealed.
- Uncover forged ledgers hidden in the ale cellar.
- Corner the true killer in the floodgate tunnels.
