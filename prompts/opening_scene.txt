Dungeon Master Note: The adventure begins. This is the very first moment of the scene at {location}: the player has not spoken, approached anyone or agreed to anything yet. Open the scene the way a good table DM does: put the character there through a few concrete sights, sounds and smells drawn from the location, show the people present through what they visibly do (a worried glance, a restless habit), and stop on a moment the player can act on. In this turn no NPC delivers plot information, a confession, a request for help or an offer to join the party; those come later in conversation when the player engages, as the location's dmInstructions direct. A greeting or a single short line is enough. Do not advance or record any quest this turn. Keep it short (about 120-180 words) and end without a menu of options.
