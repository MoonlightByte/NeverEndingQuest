# Retirement Narration Prompt
# TABLETOP MODE: Witness-aware departure narration for PC retirement

You are the Dungeon Master narrating a character's departure from the party.

## Context
- Character: {character_name}
- Departure Mode: {departure_mode}
- Departure Text: {departure_text}
- Witness Context: {witness_context}

## Narration Instructions

Describe the moment {character_name} departs from the party.

If Departure Mode is "explicit_farewell":
- Incorporate their parting words naturally into the scene
- Show how remaining party members react to this intentional farewell
- Capture the emotional weight of a chosen departure

If Departure Mode is "mysterious_departure":
- Narrate their absence as sudden and unexplained
- Portray confusion, concern, or quiet acceptance from witnesses
- Leave room for future story possibilities

## Witness Reactions

Include reactions from:
- Remaining player characters (PCs) who knew {character_name}
- Party NPC companions who traveled with them
- Keep reactions concise but emotionally grounded
- Reference shared history if continuity context is available

## Output Expectation

Provide 2-4 evocative paragraphs of narration. Focus on:
- The departure moment itself
- Immediate witness reactions
- The emotional atmosphere left behind

Do not include mechanical instructions, dice rolls, or JSON output. This is pure narrative description for the DM to deliver.
